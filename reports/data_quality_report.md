Applying the illustrative grading rules in `configs/curriculum.yaml` (pass = total ≥ 40 % and ETE ≥ 40 %; 10-point grade scale) to the semester-5 marks gives an SGPA for each student, which can be compared with the file's own `prior_sgpa`:

|---|---|---|
| mean | 7.19 | 5.52 |

| 10th percentile | 5.74 | 1.63 |

| 50th percentile | 7.19 | 6.04 |
| 75th percentile | 8.03 | 7.83 |
| 90th percentile | 8.59 | 8.70 |

- Under the rule *fail ≥ 1 subject OR SGPA < 6*, **66.0 %** of the 200 determinable students would be 'in difficulty' (131 fail at least one subject).
- **Internal inconsistency:** the previous semester's SGPA is tightly clustered (sd 1.11) while this semester's marks imply a far wider spread (sd 2.65). Among the 64 students whose prior SGPA is between 6.5 and 7.5, 84 % fail at least one subject this semester. Real cohorts usually change less abruptly from one semester to the next.
- **Consequence for Phase 4:** the synthetic generator copies this file's *schema* (columns, subjects, credits, schemes, value formats) but not its *spread*. It generates every semester from one consistent process, so each `prior_sgpa` is exactly the SGPA computed from the previous semester's marks.

## 11. Timing of each field and leakage review

| field | when it is known | use for the next-semester target |
|---|---|---|
| `prior_sgpa` | before the semester | allowed |
| attendance (subject and overall) | during the semester | allowed |
| CIE marks | during the semester | allowed |
| termwork marks | end of the semester | allowed |
| ETE marks, subject totals | end of the semester | allowed |
| `credit_weighted_marks_pct` | end of the semester | allowed |
| `study_hours_per_week`, `part_time_job`, `commute_time_mins` | self-reported | allowed after review (Phase 5) |
| `gender`, `age` | static | excluded by default |
| `PRN`, `student_name` | — | never |
| anything from semester *t + 1* | the future | **never** (that is the label) |

For the chosen task (end of semester *t* → difficulty in semester *t + 1*), every semester-*t* column is legitimately known at prediction time. ETE and totals would be **leakage** only for an *in-semester* early-warning variant that predicts semester *t*'s own outcome; the code records this timing in `SUBJECT_FIELD_ROLES` (`src/scholartrack/data/schema.py`).
