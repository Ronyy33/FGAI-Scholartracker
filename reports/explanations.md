## Concept-group effects for two groups of validation students

| model | group | students | n | mean effect (points) | share where it raises risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| logistic_regression | Current-semester results | with a failed subject | 109 | 56.28 | 0.94 |
| logistic_regression | Attendance | with attendance < 75 % | 283 | -4.34 | 0 |
| mlp | Current-semester results | with a failed subject | 109 | 55.74 | 0.96 |
| mlp | Attendance | with attendance < 75 % | 283 | -4.58 | 0.01 |

Grades behave as expected: for students who failed a subject, the results group raises the estimate in almost every case. Attendance does **not**: given the same grades, students with lower attendance are estimated as slightly *less* likely to struggle. In the synthetic generator engagement drives both attendance and marks, so among students with equal marks, lower attendance signals higher underlying ability (*explaining away*). The models faithfully reflect this; it is an association, not advice. The app shows it honestly with a note, and attendance suggestions come from the attendance guideline (`configs/recommendations.yaml`), not from the model.
