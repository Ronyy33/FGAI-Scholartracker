**Reading this comparison.**

- *Same:* columns, subject codes, credits, assessment schemes and value formats.
- *Close:* attendance and study-hours levels and spreads, part-time-job share, and the correlations of prior SGPA, attendance, study hours and commute time with marks.
- *Prior SGPA:* the mean is close, but the synthetic spread is wider (sd 1.43 vs 1.11). The cause is failed subjects, which earn 0 grade points: among synthetic students with no failure in semester 4 (n = 171) the sd is 0.99 and the minimum 5.00; the 25 students with a failure form the low tail. The reference's minimum prior SGPA (4.02) suggests few failures or a different SGPA convention in its previous semester.
- *Deliberately different:* how widely **current** marks are spread. The reference's semester-5 marks imply a much lower and wider SGPA than its own `prior_sgpa` (`reports/data_quality_report.md` §10); the generator keeps consecutive semesters consistent instead, so its difficulty rate is far lower.
- `gender`, `age` and `commute_time_mins` are drawn independently of everything else and have **no effect** on any outcome; they exist only for schema compatibility.
