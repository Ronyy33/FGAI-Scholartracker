# Data Dictionary

> Generated by `scripts/build_data_dictionary.py` from `src/scholartrack/data/schema.py`, `src/scholartrack/features/specs.py`, `configs/features.yaml` and `configs/target.yaml`. Do not edit by hand.

Three layers: **raw fields** as they appear in a college-format CSV, **engineered features** the models actually receive, and the **label**. A row of the modelling table is *one student at the end of semester t*; every feature uses information from semesters ≤ t only.

## 1. Raw student-level fields

| field | meaning | type | unit | permitted | role | when known | direct model input |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `PRN` | Permanent registration number (direct identifier) | text | — | free text | identifier | always | no — direct identifier |
| `student_name` | Student name (direct identifier) | text | — | free text | identifier | always | no — direct identifier |
| `class_division` | Class and division, e.g. T.Y.1 | text | — | pattern `^[A-Z]{1,2}\.Y\.\d+$` | context | start of semester | no — administrative grouping |
| `academic_year` | Academic year, e.g. 2026-27 | text | — | pattern `^\d{4}-\d{2}$` | context | start of semester | no — calendar position; would let a model learn year effects that do not transfer |
| `semester` | Semester number of this record | integer | — | 1–8 | context | start of semester | yes |
| `elective_subject` | Chosen programme elective (values checked against the curriculum) | categorical | — | free text | context | start of semester | no — a course choice, not a risk signal |
| `age` | Age in years | integer | years | 15–40 | sensitive | always | no — sensitive attribute; little academic meaning within one programme year |

## 2. Raw subject-level fields

Each subject contributes columns named `<CODE>_<field>`, e.g. `CE24PCC301_CIE_marks`. Subject codes, credits, schemes and maxima come from `configs/curriculum.yaml`. Subject fields are never model inputs directly; they are summarised into the features of §3.

| column | meaning | type | unit | permitted | when known |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `<CODE>_attendance_pct` | Attendance in the subject | float | % | 0–100 | during semester |
| `<CODE>_CIE_marks` | Continuous internal evaluation marks (theory subjects; 0 / blank for ETE-only subjects) | integer | marks | 0–CIE max (40 for 100-mark theory) | during semester |
| `<CODE>_ETE_marks` | End-term examination marks | integer | marks | 0–ETE max (60 for 100-mark theory; full marks for ETE-only) | end of semester |
| `<CODE>_termwork_marks` | Termwork marks (termwork-only subjects) | integer | marks | 0–max marks | end of semester |
| `<CODE>_total_marks` | Subject total (CIE + ETE, ETE, or termwork) | integer | marks | 0–max marks | end of semester |
| `<CODE>_max_marks` | Maximum marks of the subject | integer | marks | must equal the curriculum value | always |

## 3. Engineered features (model inputs)

| feature | meaning | type | unit | range | known at | built from | used | missing indicator |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `semester` | Semester number t of the record | integer | semester | 1–8 | start of semester t | semester | yes | no |
| `prior_sgpa` | SGPA of semester t − 1 as reported in the record | float | SGPA | 0–10 (missing in semester 1) | start of semester t | prior_sgpa | yes | yes |
| `sgpa_current` | SGPA of semester t computed from subject totals with the grading rules (credit-weighted grade points over subjects whose result is known) | float | SGPA | 0–10 | end of semester t | subject totals, ETE marks, credits | yes | yes |
| `sgpa_change` | sgpa_current − prior_sgpa (momentum) | float | SGPA | −10–10 | end of semester t | sgpa_current, prior_sgpa | yes | no |
| `credit_weighted_pct` | Credit-weighted mean of subject total percentages in semester t | float | % | 0–100 | end of semester t | subject totals, max marks, credits | yes | no |
| `n_failed` | Number of subjects failed in semester t (pass rule in configs/curriculum.yaml) | integer | subjects | 0–11 | end of semester t | subject totals, ETE marks | yes | no |
| `share_subjects_below_50` | Share of semester-t subjects whose total is below 50 % | float | fraction | 0–1 | end of semester t | subject totals | yes | no |
| `min_subject_pct` | Lowest subject total percentage in semester t | float | % | 0–100 | end of semester t | subject totals | yes | no |
| `cie_mean_pct` | Mean CIE percentage over theory subjects in semester t | float | % | 0–100 | during semester t | CIE marks | yes | no |
| `ete_mean_pct` | Mean ETE percentage over subjects with an end-term exam in semester t | float | % | 0–100 | end of semester t | ETE marks | yes | no |
| `termwork_mean_pct` | Mean termwork percentage over termwork subjects in semester t | float | % | 0–100 | end of semester t | termwork marks | yes | no |
| `attendance_pct` | Credit-weighted mean subject attendance in semester t (recomputed) | float | % | 0–100 | during semester t | subject attendance, credits | yes | no |
| `min_subject_attendance` | Lowest subject attendance in semester t | float | % | 0–100 | during semester t | subject attendance | yes | no |
| `share_subjects_attendance_below_75` | Share of semester-t subjects with attendance below 75 % | float | fraction | 0–1 | during semester t | subject attendance | yes | no |
| `study_hours_per_week` | Self-reported weekly study hours in semester t | float | hours/week | 0–100 | during semester t (self-reported) | study_hours_per_week | yes | yes |

- Thresholds inside features (illustrative): low marks < 50 %, low attendance < 75 %.
- Missing values: a `<feature>_missing` indicator (0/1) is added where marked, then the value is imputed with the **training** median and standardised with the **training** mean and standard deviation (`src/scholartrack/features/preprocess.py`).
- The **MLP** and **logistic regression** receive these 15 features (+ 3 indicators) for semester t. The **LSTM** receives the same vector for each of the student's semesters 1…t, in time order (at most 8).

## 4. Label

| name | meaning | type | known at | model input | definition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `label` | 1 if semester t + 1 is an academic-difficulty semester: ≥ 1 failed subject or SGPA < 6; 0 otherwise | 0 / 1 (missing when undeterminable) | end of semester t + 1 | never | `docs/TARGET_DEFINITION.md`, `configs/target.yaml` |

`label_status` records why a row has no label (`future_semester_not_observed`, `future_outcome_undetermined`); such rows are scored but never used for training or evaluation.
